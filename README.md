# Macroeconomic Analysis and Policy

**Practice Topics in Global Economics: Macroeconomic Analysis and Policies**

This repository documents applied macroeconomic analysis and policy evaluation projects developed as part of my graduate-level coursework in Global Economics.

The portfolio covers key macroeconomic topics, including aggregate supply and demand, business cycles, inflation dynamics, monetary policy, and fiscal policy.

## Project Areas

| No. | Topic | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Macroeconomic Framework | Macroeconomic indicators and accounting frameworks |
| 02 | Aggregate Supply | GDP measurement, potential output, and growth analysis |
| 03 | Aggregate Demand | Aggregate demand and macroeconomic equilibrium |
| 04 | Inflation Analysis | Inflation measurement, core inflation, and inflation dynamics |
| 05 | Monetary Policy | Monetary policy frameworks and policy assessment |
| 06 | Fiscal Policy | Government revenue, expenditure, budget balances, and fiscal stance |
| 07 | Policy Research | Applied analysis of U.S. monetary and fiscal policy |

## Tools and Methods

- **Microsoft Excel:** Macroeconomic data processing, quantitative analysis, and modeling.
- **Economic analysis:** Aggregate supply and demand, inflation, monetary policy, and fiscal policy.
- **Data analysis:** Macroeconomic indicators, time-series data, and policy evaluation.

## Research Focus

The portfolio includes an applied analysis of U.S. monetary and fiscal policy, examining macroeconomic developments and policy decisions over the period 2005–2024.

## Repository Structure

Each project directory contains relevant working files, datasets, and analytical outputs where available.
